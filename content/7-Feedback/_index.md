### Overall Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**  
The working environment is very friendly and open. FCAJ members are always willing to help whenever I encounter difficulties, even outside working hours. The workspace is tidy and quite help me forcus on labs and project .

**2. Support from Mentor / Team Admin**  
My mentor gives thorough, clear explanations whenever I'm confused, and consistently encourages me to speak up with questions. The admin team handles administrative matters smoothly, supplies whatever documents I need, and sets things up so I can work effectively. What I value most is that my mentor lets me attempt problems on my own first, rather than simply handing me the solution.

3. **Relevance of Work to Academic Major**
The tasks I was given lined up well with what I'd studied at university, while also exposing me to areas I'd never dealt with before. This let me both reinforce what I already knew and pick up new practical skills.

4. **Learning & Skill Development Opportunities**
Over the course of the internship, I picked up several new skills — working with project management tools, collaborating effectively as a team, and communicating professionally in a corporate setting. My mentor also shared valuable real-world experience that helped me think more clearly about my career direction.

5. **Company Culture & Team Spirit**
The culture here is genuinely positive: people treat each other with respect and take the work seriously without losing a sense of enjoyment. When urgent projects come up, everyone pitches in and supports one another regardless of rank. That made me feel like a real member of the team, not just an intern passing through.

6. **Internship Policies / Benefits**
The company offers an internship allowance along with flexible working hours when needed. Being able to join internal training sessions on top of that .
